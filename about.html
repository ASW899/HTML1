Department Overview

MCA Computer Application department is one of the main departments of Mohandas college. Here we have well educated faculties and exclusive facilites that help the children achieve their peak performance.

The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) is a two-year postgraduate programme comprising four semesters, structured according to the academic regulations of APJ Abdul Kalam Technological University (KTU).
